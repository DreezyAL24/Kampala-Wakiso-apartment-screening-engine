# Kampala and Wakiso Apartment Blocks Up to UGX 800 Million

Market scan date: 11 June 2026

## What was included

- Whole apartment blocks or properties with multiple rental units.
- Asking price at or below UGX 800 million.
- Locations in Kampala District or Wakiso District.
- Publicly indexed adverts found on PropertyPro Uganda, Uganda Property Agents and the Real Estate Database.

## Important limitation

This is a broad online market scan, not a guarantee that every property is still available. Ugandan property portals contain old adverts, repeated agent postings and properties that are sold but not removed. Availability, title, rent roll, tenancy, planning approval and agent authority should be confirmed before paying an inspection fee or deposit.

I found **65 qualifying advertisement records** after removing obvious sold notices, listings above budget, individual condominiums and exact duplicate URLs. Possible cross-posted duplicates remain where descriptions differ.

## Risk Flags

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Price conflict | The card price and description price disagree. Confirm the actual asking price in writing. |
| Undated | The advert appears in an active category but has no reliable posting date. Confirm it has not sold. |
| Shell / unfinished | Budget for completion, approvals, utilities, drainage and vacancy before comparing its yield with occupied blocks. |
| Possible duplicate | Similar price, location, unit count or rent appears in another advert and may be the same property. |
| Agreement title | The advert does not state a registered title is already available. Independent legal checks are essential. |

## Excluded During Screening

- Listings explicitly marked sold or unavailable.
- Individual condominiums incorrectly categorised as apartment blocks.
- A UGX 670m Namugongo advert on LegitPlot that states it is an illustration and no real property is offered.
- USD 220,000 listings, which exceed UGX 800m at approximately UGX 3,758 per USD on 11 June 2026.
- Listings above UGX 800m.
